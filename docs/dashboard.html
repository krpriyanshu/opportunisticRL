Hypotheses

docs/evaluation.md — resolved from the executed M9–M11 comparison.

H1 partial
H1 · primary

An opportunity signal in the observation raises grasp success and/or lowers timing regret vs. an otherwise-identical reactive policy, under matched conditions.

Provisional. Split by budget: sample efficiency YES (21/24 independent low-budget units, p=2.8e-4), final performance NO (14/24, p=.54). Evidence predates four correctness fixes and is not reproducible — see docs/evaluation.md §2.
H2 · secondary

The benefit of opportunity-awareness grows with disturbance magnitude.

Undetermined. Previously reported rejected on 3 seeds; an 8-seed sample gives an ordering that increases monotonically with severity (−0.17 / +0.38 / +0.47), contradicting that verdict.
H3 · secondary

Opportunity-aware policies generalize better outside the training distribution.

Not tested (distribution-shift split not built) — open.
H4 · secondary

How much of the oracle-vs-reactive timing-regret gap does opportunity-awareness close?

Not tested (oracle baseline not built) — open.
